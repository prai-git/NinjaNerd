NinjaNerd.ai provides a free educational practice platform for students in grades 1–7. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you and, where applicable, to parents and guardians.

In this Policy, "NinjaNerd" ("we", "us", or "our") means the service provided at the domain ninjanerd.ai, operated by the owner of that domain and of the public source repository named NinjaNerd. The service is offered under several forms of the same name — NinjaNerd, NinjaNerd.ai, NINJANERD, NINJANERD.AI and ninjanerd.ai — in any capitalisation, spacing or styling. These refer to one and the same service, together with www.ninjanerd.ai and any successor or additional domain we operate.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account registration details: your email address, and a school name if you choose to enter one
  • Educational progress and performance data (questions attempted, the answer given, whether it was correct, and per-topic totals)
  • Any message you send us through our contact form, together with the email address of the account that sent it

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Your learning activity as you use the platform: the questions you attempt and the answers you give
  • The date and time of your most recent sign-in
  • Sign-in and session information handled by our authentication provider, which processes technical data such as an IP address in order to keep accounts secure

We do not run analytics or advertising, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not collect device fingerprints or location data.

We do not collect payment information — NinjaNerd is free and has no paid features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our educational services
  • Save and display each student's learning progress
  • Send important account and service updates
  • Analyze usage to improve content and the platform
  • Keep the platform secure and prevent misuse, fraud, or abuse

We do not use children's personal information for behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With service providers who help us operate the platform (Google Firebase for authentication and data storage, and EmailJS for delivering messages sent through our contact form), under obligations of confidentiality and only as needed to provide the service
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or the public
  • In connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), subject to this Policy

4. Data Security

We use reasonable measures designed to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Regular security reviews and updates

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

NinjaNerd is intended for use by children in grades 1–7 under the supervision of a parent, guardian, or school. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable laws:

  • We limit the personal information we collect from children to what is reasonably necessary to provide the educational service.
  • Where required, we obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13. An account for a child should be created or authorized by a parent, guardian, or school.
  • Parents and guardians may review the personal information we hold about their child, request that we correct or delete it, and refuse to permit further collection, by contacting us through our contact form.
  • We do not require a child to provide more information than is reasonably necessary to use the service, and we do not knowingly allow children to publicly post personal information.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

NinjaNerd does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and does not use analytics cookies.

To keep you signed in, our authentication provider stores sign-in information in your browser's local storage. We also store a small local record of when you were last active, so that the site can sign you out after a period of inactivity. Clearing your browser storage signs you out and removes both.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and update your personal information, from the Account page while you are signed in
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data, by contacting us through the contact form
  • File a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services and maintain your account
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Account data is typically deleted within 30 days after account closure, or sooner when it is no longer needed.

9. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate NinjaNerd: Google Firebase for sign-in (Authentication) and for storing learning progress (Firestore), and EmailJS to deliver messages sent through our contact form. Your use of these features is also subject to those providers' privacy policies.

10. International Transfers

If you access NinjaNerd from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries with different privacy laws. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be communicated through prominent notices on our website.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, please reach us through our contact form.

By using NinjaNerd.ai, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and use of information as described herein.
