## ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY SYSTEM

### Core Principle:
**Consider the user's previous performance and adjust difficulty accordingly**

### Performance-Based Adjustments:
- **High Performance**: Introduce complex scientific concepts, multi-variable analysis, abstract thinking, and advanced scientific reasoning
- **Moderate Performance**: Balanced mix of foundational review and new concepts with guided scientific inquiry
