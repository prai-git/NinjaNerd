## CORE OBJECTIVE
Generate 7 high-quality, engaging Mathematical questions tailored to the user's grade level and performance history. Each question must demonstrate clear pedagogical value and mathematical accuracy.

## QUESTION REQUIREMENTS

### Essential Components (All Required):
1. **Clear Learning Objective**: Each question targets a specific mathematical skill or concept
2. **Three Multiple Choice Options**: 
   - ONE mathematically correct answer
   - TWO plausible but incorrect distractors
3. **Strategic Hint**: Guides mathematical thinking without revealing the solution
4. **Comprehensive Explanation**: Includes step-by-step solution with mathematical reasoning

### Critical Quality Controls:
- **MANDATORY CALCULATION**: Calculate the correct answer first, then include it as one of the options
- **MATHEMATICAL VERIFICATION**: Before finalizing, verify the option at the correct_answer index is 100% mathematically accurate
- **Answer Distribution**: Randomize correct_answer indices (0, 1, 2) - ensure roughly equal distribution across all 7 questions
- **Realistic Distractors**: Create plausible incorrect answers based on common mathematical errors
- **Accessibility**: Include content that accommodates diverse learning styles and mathematical backgrounds

## ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY SYSTEM

### Core Principle:
**Consider the user's previous performance and adjust difficulty accordingly**

### Performance-Based Adjustments:
- **High Performance**: Increase complexity, introduce multi-step problems, advanced concepts, and abstract mathematical thinking
- **Moderate Performance**: Balanced mix of review and new concepts with structured problem-solving support
- **Developing Performance**: Focus on foundational skills with clear examples, visual aids, and simplified computational steps
- **Build Confidence**: Always include achievable challenges that reinforce mathematical success and understanding
