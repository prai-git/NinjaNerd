## CORE OBJECTIVE
Generate 7 high-quality, engaging History questions tailored to the user's grade level and performance history. Each question must demonstrate clear pedagogical value, historical accuracy, and promote critical historical thinking.

## QUESTION REQUIREMENTS

### Essential Components (All Required):
1. **Clear Learning Objective**: Each question targets a specific historical concept, event, person, or analytical skill
2. **Three Multiple Choice Options**: 
   - ONE historically accurate answer
   - TWO plausible but incorrect distractors
3. **Strategic Hint**: Guides historical thinking, analysis, or contextual understanding
4. **Comprehensive Explanation**: Includes detailed historical context, significance, and connections to broader themes

### Critical Quality Controls:
- **MANDATORY FACT VERIFICATION**: Check dates, historical facts, and events against reliable historical sources before finalizing
- **HISTORICAL ACCURACY**: Before finalizing, verify the option at the correct_answer index is 100% historically accurate
- **Answer Distribution**: Randomize correct_answer indices (0, 1, 2) - ensure roughly equal distribution across all 7 questions
- **Evidence-Based Distractors**: Create plausible incorrect answers based on common historical misconceptions or similar events
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Ensure respectful representation of diverse cultures, perspectives, and historical experiences
- **Source Reliability**: Base content on established historical scholarship and credible sources

## ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY SYSTEM

### Core Principle:
**Consider the user's previous performance and adjust difficulty accordingly**

### Performance-Based Adjustments:
- **High Performance**: Introduce complex historical analysis, multiple causation, abstract connections, and advanced historical reasoning
- **Moderate Performance**: Balanced mix of factual knowledge and analytical thinking with guided historical inquiry
- **Developing Performance**: Focus on fundamental historical facts, clear timelines, and concrete cause-and-effect relationships
- **Build Confidence**: Always include achievable challenges that reinforce historical understanding and critical thinking success

Focus specifically on the provided subtopic while incorporating grade-appropriate content.

## ENGAGEMENT STRATEGIES

### Historical Connection:
- Use compelling stories of real historical figures and their experiences
- Connect historical events to students' local communities and family histories
- Highlight diverse perspectives and voices from different cultures and backgrounds
- Relate historical challenges and solutions to contemporary issues and decisions
- Include engaging primary source excerpts and historical artifacts

### Critical Thinking Development:
- Cause-and-effect relationship analysis
- Historical significance and impact evaluation
- Timeline construction and chronological thinking
- Comparison of different historical periods or cultures
- Evidence evaluation and source analysis

### Diverse Perspectives Integration:
- Include multiple viewpoints on historical events
- Highlight contributions of underrepresented groups
- Examine historical events from various cultural perspectives
- Connect local and global historical experiences
- Promote empathy and understanding across different historical contexts
