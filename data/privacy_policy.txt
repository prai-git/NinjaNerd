NINJANERD.AI PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

NinjaNerd.ai ("NinjaNerd", "we", "us", or "our") provides a FREE educational practice platform for students in grades 1-7. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you and, where applicable, to parents and guardians.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Information You Provide
- Account registration details (email address and username)
- Educational progress and performance data (questions attempted, answers, scores)
- Any message you send us through our contact form

1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- Usage data and learning activity
- Device and browser information
- IP address and general (approximate) location
- Session and interaction data

We do NOT collect payment information - NinjaNerd is free and has no paid features.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve our educational services
- Save and display each student's learning progress
- Send important account and service updates
- Analyze usage to improve content and the platform
- Keep the platform secure and prevent misuse, fraud, or abuse

We do not use children's personal information for behavioral or targeted advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:
- With service providers who help us operate the platform (for example, authentication and data-storage providers such as Google Firebase), under obligations of confidentiality and only as needed to provide the service
- When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or the public
- In connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), subject to this Policy

4. DATA SECURITY

We use reasonable measures designed to protect your information, including:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication systems
- Regular security reviews and updates

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

NinjaNerd is intended for use by children in grades 1-7 under the supervision of a parent, guardian, or school. We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable laws:
- We limit the personal information we collect from children to what is reasonably necessary to provide the educational service.
- Where required, we obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13. An account for a child should be created or authorized by a parent, guardian, or school.
- Parents and guardians may review the personal information we hold about their child, request that we correct or delete it, and refuse to permit further collection, by contacting us through our contact form.
- We do not require a child to provide more information than is reasonably necessary to use the service, and we do not knowingly allow children to publicly post personal information.

If you believe a child has provided us personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Keep you signed in and remember your preferences
- Analyze platform usage and performance

You can control cookies through your browser settings. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You have the right to:
- Access and update your personal information
- Request deletion of your account and associated data
- File a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority

8. DATA RETENTION

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to:
- Provide our services and maintain your account
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Account data is typically deleted within 30 days after account closure, or sooner when it is no longer needed.

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate NinjaNerd, including Google Firebase for sign-in (Authentication) and for storing learning progress (Firestore). Your use of these features is also subject to those providers' privacy policies.

10. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

If you access NinjaNerd from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other countries with different privacy laws. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

11. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on the Privacy Policy page and revise the "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be communicated through prominent notices on our website.

12. CONTACT US

For privacy questions or requests, please reach us through the contact form available on the platform.

By using NinjaNerd.ai, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and use of information as described herein.
